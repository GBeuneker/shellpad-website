## Description

A 2D co-op action RPG roguelike inspired by hollow knight. Defeat monsters, and collect gear to get stronger and reach further into a randomly generated dungeon.

## Motivation

I really wanted to make a 2D action game RPG similar to hollow-knight that you could play with friends through online multiplayer. I imagined it as a roguelike so you can have endless replayability.

## Details

**Framework:** Unity  
**Language:** C#
