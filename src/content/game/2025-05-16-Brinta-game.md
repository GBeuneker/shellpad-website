## Description

An incremental game where you slowly build your Brinta empire. Buy upgrades to increase your yield and workers to mine your precious Brinta and try to reach the end!

## Motivation

I made this game as a wedding gift for a friend of mine. It was sort of an inside joke that got out of hand.

## Details

**Framework:** Unity  
**Language:** C#
